Graph Pathfinder is an interactive playground for shortest-path search. Draw
walls, drop weighted terrain, and run A* or Dijkstra to watch the frontier
expand in real time.

Side-by-side runs make the tradeoffs concrete: how the heuristic reshapes A*'s
search, where Dijkstra spends its effort, and what changes when edges aren't
uniform.
